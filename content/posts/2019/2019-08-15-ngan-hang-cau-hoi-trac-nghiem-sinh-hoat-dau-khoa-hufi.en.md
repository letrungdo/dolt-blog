The HUFI Orientation Week multiple-choice question bank includes 14 chapters containing nearly 400 quiz questions for HUFI students preparing for the orientation course final exam.

- **Part 1:** Overview information about the university
- **Part 2:** Academic training information
- **Part 3:** Student regulations and study rules
- **Part 4:** Conduct evaluation regulations
- **Part 5:** Policy and benefits regulations
- **Part 6:** Regulations on Vocational Education & Social Work courses
- **Part 7:** Off-campus residency regulations
- **Part 8:** School security and order regulations
- **Part 9:** Youth Union work & youth movement activities
- **Part 10:** Library information and regulations
- **Part 11:** Political and current affairs information
- **Part 12:** Traffic safety information and regulations
- **Part 13:** Drug and HIV/AIDS prevention information
- **Part 14:** Learning Ho Chi Minh's moral example
