When Japan paid your **pension lump-sum withdrawal payment** (脱退一時金), it **withheld 20.42% income tax** from the Employees' Pension (厚生年金) part. You can ask the Japanese tax office (税務署) to **refund that tax**. Many people call this the "second nenkin refund". Most people get back almost all of it.

This guide shows how to do it **yourself** after you have left Japan, without paying an agency:

- Estimate your refund before you file.
- Ask someone in Japan to be your **tax agent (納税管理人)**.
- Fill in the **new form (令和7年分以降用)**, required for returns filed from **September 24, 2026**. You get **highlighted samples** of every field and links to the original forms.

## 1. Can you claim the refund?

You can if all three are true:

1. You have received the lump-sum payment and still have the **original 脱退一時金支給決定通知書** (Notice of Entitlement). It shows the payment amount and the tax withheld.
2. **Someone living in Japan** agrees to be your tax agent. A relative, friend or former coworker is fine. No qualification is needed.
3. It has been less than **5 years**. Once the payment amount is fixed (after the 支給決定 date), you can **file right away**. You do not have to wait until the next year.

> 💡 Tax is only withheld from the **Employees' Pension (厚生年金)** part. If you only paid into the National Pension (国民年金), no tax was withheld, so there is nothing to refund.

### Why do you need a tax agent?

- The tax office requires someone living abroad to have a **納税管理人** in Japan who receives letters and handles the procedure for them.
- The refund **can only be paid into a Japanese bank account**, so it goes to the agent's account and the agent sends it on to you.
- **The agent pays no tax on it.** The refund is your tax money, in your name. It is not the agent's income, and they do not need to report anything.
- If you did not appoint an agent before leaving Japan, you can still do it now. Send the appointment form (Form 1 below) together with the tax return.

## 2. Estimate your refund

Take three numbers from the **脱退一時金支給決定通知書**, in the **厚生年金保険 (Employees' Pension Insurance)** box:

| On the notice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 支給額 | Payment amount (before tax) |
| 所得税額及び復興特別所得税額 | Tax withheld (20.42%) |
| 被保険者期間 | Months of coverage |

**How to calculate:**

1. **Years** = months ÷ 12, **rounded up** (e.g. 30 months → 3 years).
2. **Retirement income deduction (退職所得控除額)** = ¥400,000 × years, **at least ¥800,000**.
3. If **支給額 ≤ deduction**, you get **all of the withheld tax back**.
4. If **支給額 > deduction**, you still owe a little tax:
   - Taxable income = (支給額 − deduction) ÷ 2, rounded down to the nearest ¥1,000.
   - Tax = taxable income × 5% (if under ¥1,950,000), plus 2.1% reconstruction tax.
   - **Refund = tax withheld − tax owed.**

**Example (fake numbers, used in the samples below):** 支給額 ¥1,500,000, ¥306,300 withheld, 36 months of coverage.

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Years | 36 months | 3 years |
| Deduction | 400,000 × 3 | 1,200,000 |
| Taxable income | (1,500,000 − 1,200,000) ÷ 2 | 150,000 |
| Income tax (所得税) | 150,000 × 5% | 7,500 |
| Reconstruction tax (復興特別所得税) | 7,500 × 2.1%, fractions dropped | 157 |
| Tax owed | 7,500 + 157 | 7,657 |
| **Refund** | 306,300 − 7,657 | **¥298,643** (≈ 97%) |

### What changed in the new form?

Returns filed **from September 24, 2026** must use the 令和7年分以降用 form. It uses **number codes** instead of circling or writing words:

| Field | Write |
|---|---|
| Date of birth – era (第一表) | **4** = Heisei (平成), 3 = Showa, 5 = Reiwa |
| 分離 G04 | **1** (because you use 第三表) |
| Institution type Z02 | **1** bank · 2 金庫 · 3 組合 · 4 農協 · 5 漁協 |
| Head/branch office Z04 | 1 head office · **2 branch** · 3 本所 · 4 支所 · 5 sub-branch |
| Account type Z05 | **1** ordinary (普通) · 2 current · 6 savings |
| Special provision – law G01 (第三表) | **1** = Income Tax Act |

### Rules for filling in

- Write by hand with a **black ballpoint pen**. Do not use blue or purple ink, or erasable pens (Frixion).
- **Typing and printing is fine.** Print on **A4 at 100% scale**, one side only.
- To correct a mistake, **draw two lines** through it and write above. Do not use correction fluid.
- Neither form has a **signature or seal** box.

## 5. Form 2: Tax return (確定申告書)

On **all 3 pages**:
- In 「令和□年分」, write the year of the **支給決定年月日** (entitlement date) on your Notice of Entitlement (2025 → 7, 2026 → 8), not the year you file.
- **Strike out** 「所得税及び復興特別所得税」.
- Write **「退職所得の選択課税」** in the blank space of the title.

## 6. Where to submit

- Submit to the **tax office for your last address in Japan**. Find it on the <a href="https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/access/map.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">NTA tax office list</a>.
- **In person:** the agent takes the documents to that tax office's counter.
- **By mail:** ⚠️ many tax offices (especially in Tokyo) want mail sent to a **業務センター** (processing center), not to the tax office address. Check the <a href="https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/tokyo/shokai/submissionprocessingcenter.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Tokyo Regional Taxation Bureau processing center list</a>, or call the tax office first. Send it by **簡易書留** (simple registered mail) so it can be tracked.

### What goes in the envelope

1. Form 1: 納税管理人届出書
2. Form 2: 第一表, 第二表, 第三表
3. The **original** 脱退一時金支給決定通知書 (it replaces the 源泉徴収票). The remittance notice 送金通知書 is not needed.
4. Copy of your passport: the photo page and the page with the Japan departure stamp.
5. Copy of your residence card (both sides).

> 📸 **Take photos of everything** before sending. The refund usually reaches the agent's account in **2–4 months**.

## 7. FAQ

**I returned my My Number card when I left. What do I write?**
Leave 個人番号 blank. The copies of your passport and residence card serve as ID. If the tax office needs more, they will contact your agent.

**What number goes in G01 at the top of 第一表?**
The new form has only 4 codes: 1 確定 (regular), 2 修正 (amended), 3 準確定 (death or departure), 4 amended 準確定. None of them is specifically for 退職所得の選択課税. The sample **leaves G01 blank** and writes 「退職所得の選択課税」 in the blank space next to it, following the note printed on the form. Your agent can ask the tax office: 「退職所得の選択課税の申告書の場合、G01欄は何番ですか」.

**I paid in for less than 5 years. Do I use the 一般 or 短期 row on 第三表?**
The sample uses the **一般** row, which is how the NTA fills it in for the lump-sum payment. For normal lump-sum amounts, both rows give the same tax.

**What if my calculation is wrong?**
The return is not rejected. The tax office checks it, corrects the refund amount and sends a notice to your agent.
