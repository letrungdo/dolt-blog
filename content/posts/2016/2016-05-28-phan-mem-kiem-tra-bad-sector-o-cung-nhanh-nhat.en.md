  This utility evaluates hard drive health and bad sector warnings instantly. Opening the application displays drive status within 5 seconds.
</p>

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-2012" src="/media/2016/05/Crystal-Disk-Info-6.5.png" alt="" width="674" height="532" />

CrystalDiskInfo reports 4 status results:

1. Good - Blue: Drive is healthy with no bad sectors.
2. Caution - Yellow: Drive has errors or minor bad sectors that can be remapped/repaired with software.
3. Bad - Red: Hard drive has severe bad sectors that software cannot repair; damaged partitions must be isolated.
4. Gray - Gray: Status unknown.

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  This guide is <strong>extremely useful when buying used laptops</strong>; testing drive health with CrystalDiskInfo takes only 5 seconds.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">

</p>
