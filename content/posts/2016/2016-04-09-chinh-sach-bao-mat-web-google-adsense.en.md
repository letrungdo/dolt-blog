<p style="text-align: justify;">
  When establishing a digital brand or website, possessing a distinct Privacy Policy representing legal compliance is essential. Every professional website includes a Privacy Policy page.
</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify;">
  Why is a Privacy Policy Required?
</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Google AdSense Terms and Conditions state that all publishers participating in the program must display a clear Privacy Policy on their website.
</p>
