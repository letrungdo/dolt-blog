</p>

## Guide to Viewing HUIT Student Avatars

Reference: **<a href="/xem-diem-sinh-vien-hufi/#tra-c%E1%BB%A9u-th%C3%B4ng-tin-sinh-vi%C3%AAn-huit">Search HUIT Student Information</a>**

Once your search result appears on the portal, click on the **Student ID link**. Detailed profile info along with the avatar picture will display.

Right-click to save the image file or drag-and-drop the image onto your desktop.

Please respect privacy and do not re-publish friends' private photos on public social networks.
