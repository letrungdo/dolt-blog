When using <a href="/email-ao-la-gi-dang-ky-tai-khoan-rac/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">temporary/disposable email</a> addresses to register online accounts like Facebook, what happens if you trigger a security checkpoint or forget your password later? How can you recover a previously created temporary email address? This article guides you on how to recover temporary email addresses from 10minutemail.net.

# Recovering Temporary Email Addresses on 10minutemail.net

**Note**: Temporary email addresses are stored in your browser history and local storage. Therefore, if you clear your browser history/cookies, previously generated emails cannot be restored.

To find a previous email address, visit: <a href="https://10minutemail.net/history.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">10minutemail.net/history.html</a>

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1920" src="/media/2016/07/mail-10-phut.png" alt="" width="763" height="425" srcset="/media/2016/07/mail-10-phut.png 763w, /media/2016/07/mail-10-phut-215x120.png 215w, /media/2016/07/mail-10-phut-300x168.png 300w, /media/2016/07/mail-10-phut-414x232.png 414w" sizes="(max-width: 763px) 100vw, 763px" />

1. Select the domain matching your email suffix (after `@`).
2. Enter your email prefix into the search box.
3. Once you locate the target email address, click the arrow button shown at position 3 in the image.

That's it! The email address is regenerated. Note that previous inbox messages will be cleared as it initializes a fresh inbox instance.

P.S.: Some other temporary mail services offer similar recovery history features.
