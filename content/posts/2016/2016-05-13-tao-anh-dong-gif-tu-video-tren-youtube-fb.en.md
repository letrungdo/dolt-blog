<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Gifyoutube.com (rebranded to <strong>gifs.com</strong>) is an online tool to <strong>create animated GIFs</strong> from videos hosted on YouTube, Instagram, Vine, etc. This site allows creating animated GIFs from online videos quickly in a few simple steps to share with friends.
</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify;">
  <strong>1. Create Animated GIFs Easily from YouTube Videos</strong>
</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  <strong>Step 1:</strong> Go to YouTube and search for your desired video. Note that the tool supports video inputs up to 1 hour in length.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  <strong>Step 2</strong>: In the browser address bar, insert <strong>gif</strong> directly before <strong>youtube.com/</strong> and press Enter.
</p>

<img class="aligncenter size-full" src="/media/2016/05/gifiu-2.png" alt=""/>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  <strong>Step 3</strong>: You will be redirected to the main GIF creation editor:
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  <img class="aligncenter size-full" src="/media/2016/05/gif-1.png" alt="" />
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Drag the timeline slider to set start and end timestamps, then click <strong>Create Gif</strong>.
</p>

<strong>Step 4</strong>: Your generated GIF will be hosted on gifs.com.

<img class="aligncenter size-full" src="/media/2016/05/gif-save.png" alt="" />

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  You can copy the GIF URL to share on social networks, or download the image file locally by clicking <strong>Download GIF</strong>.
</p>

ネットワーク
## <strong>2. Create Animated GIFs from Instagram, Facebook, Vine Videos...</strong>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Navigate directly to gifs.com and paste your target video URL into the search box.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  It parses video sources automatically. Then follow Step 3 above.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  List of video platforms supported by gifs.com:
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook, Vine, Instagram, Giphy, Imgur, CNET, Flickr, IMDb, and many others.
</p>
