<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Before detailing the registration steps, what is <strong>Google AdSense for Content</strong>?
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  <a href="/phan-biet-tai-khoan-google-adsense/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google AdSense</a> for Content (AdSense for Content - AFC) is an AdSense publisher account approved through a custom website rather than YouTube or Blogspot hosted accounts. Non-hosted AdSense content accounts are harder to obtain, but ad formats are versatile and produce higher revenue potential.
</p>

## 1. Preparing Your Website for Google AdSense Registration

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  You can build a website using <strong>WordPress</strong> quickly without programming knowledge.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Host your site using custom hosting or <a href="/huong-dan-tao-hosting-openshift-cai-wordpress-toan-tap/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">free OpenShift hosting</a>.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Requirements: Clean theme design, clear navigation structure, and mobile-friendly layout.
</p>

Using top-level domain extensions like `.com` improves approval odds. Free top-level domain extensions from platforms like <a href="https://my.freenom.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">my.freenom.com</a> or `.vn` domains also work effectively.

## 2. Content Preparation for Google AdSense

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Focus on original, high-quality content.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Content must adhere to AdSense policies: no copyright violations, hate speech, adult content, or violence. Custom images and original illustrations are recommended.
</p>

## 3. Maintaining Regular Publishing Frequency

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Publish around 4 articles per week. Accumulating at least 20 well-structured articles before submitting your application is ideal.
</p>

## 4. Essential Pages and Integrations

<h3 style="text-align: justify;">
  Create <strong>About Us</strong> and <strong>Privacy Policy</strong> Pages
</h3>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Google AdSense policy strictly requires all publisher websites to feature a compliant "Privacy Policy" page.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Refer to this guide to generate a compliant privacy policy page: <a href="/chinh-sach-bao-mat-web-google-adsense" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Creating a Privacy Policy Page for Website</a>.
active
</p>

### Integrate **Google Analytics**:

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Link Google Analytics using the same Google account intended for AdSense registration.
</p>

### Register Site in Google Search Console:

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Add your domain property in <a class="goog-inline-block product-name-logo" href="https://www.google.com/webmasters/tools/home?hl=vi">Search Console</a>, verify ownership, and submit your XML sitemap. Ensure `robots.txt` does not block Googlebot crawler agents.
</p>

### Traffic Requirements

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Aim for organic/referral traffic of at least <strong>100 – 300 visits/day</strong>. Google evaluates site traffic metrics during review.
</p>

## 5. Submitting Your Google AdSense Application

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Go to AdSense homepage: <a href="https://www.google.com/adsense/app#main/home" target="_blank" rel="noopener">google.com/adsense/app#main/home</a> and log in with your Google account linked to <strong>Google Analytics and <a class="goog-inline-block product-name-logo" href="https://www.google.com/webmasters/tools/home?hl=vi">Search Console</a></strong>.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Create 1–2 ad units, copy the code snippet, and paste it into your site layout.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Under Account Settings, add your site URL to Site Management.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Wait for Google AdSense verification email notification (typically 1–3 days).
</p>
