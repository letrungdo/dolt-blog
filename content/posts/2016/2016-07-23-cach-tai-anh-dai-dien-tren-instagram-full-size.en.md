Browsing through <a href="/tag/instagram" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Instagram</a>, you often find beautiful profile pictures of friends. But seeing them in small dimensions isn't satisfying; I tried modifying the image link and succeeded. Here is how to **download profile pictures** on Instagram in **FULL SIZE**.

## Guide to Downloading Instagram Profile Pictures in FULL SIZE

1. Visit the profile page whose picture you want to download, **right-click on the avatar**, and select **Inspect**.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1904" src="/media/2016/07/avata_instagram.png" alt="" width="716" height="366" />

2. A panel appears; double-click on the link following `src="` and select **Copy**.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1905" src="/media/2016/07/avata_instagram1.png" alt="" width="748" height="352" />

You will get a link like this:

```bash
https://igcdn-photos-g-a.akamaihd.net/hphotos-ak-xtp1/t51.2885-19/s150x150/13549338_966247793492846_725150544_a.jpg
```

Remove the string `"s150x150/"` from the URL above. Paste the remaining link into your browser to view and download the profile picture in its original full size.
