<p style="text-align: justify;">
  When ISPs block Facebook at network level, simply changing DNS to Google DNS or OpenDNS may not restore access. Here are several methods to access Facebook easily.
</p>

# How to Access Facebook When Blocked?

## 1. Download Coc Coc or Tor Browser

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  + <strong>Coc Coc</strong>: Built on Chromium with built-in unblocking features for local networks in Vietnam. <a href="https://coccoc.com">Download</a> and install to access Facebook directly without extra setup. Certified safe by Norton Security.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  + <strong>Tor Browser</strong>: High security browser built on onion routing network. Bypasses blocks and maintains online anonymity. Download Tor at: <a href="https://www.torproject.org">https://www.torproject.org</a>.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Note: Tor routing can slow down loading speeds and clears cookies upon closing.
</p>

## 2. Use Unlimited Free VPN - Hola

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  For Chrome users: Install extension <a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/unlimited-free-vpn-hola/gkojfkhlekighikafcpjkiklfbnlmeio">Unlimited Free VPN - Hola</a>.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Click link above, click Add to Chrome.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Once installed, open Facebook and toggle Hola extension from browser top-right menu to switch country proxy node.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  <img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-2073" src="/media/2016/05/turn-on-hola.png" alt="" width="430" height="312" srcset="/media/2016/05/turn-on-hola.png 430w, /media/2016/05/turn-on-hola-74x55.png 74w" sizes="(max-width: 430px) 100vw, 430px" />
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Hola also provides mobile apps for <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.hola">Android</a> and <a href="https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/hola-privacy-vpn-app-browser/id903869356?mt=8">iOS</a>.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  <strong>Caution:</strong> Hola operates on a <strong>peer-to-peer</strong> bandwidth-sharing network architecture. Joining converts your device into an exit/relay node for other peers.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Avoid using on mobile cellular data to prevent unexpected data consumption.
</p>
