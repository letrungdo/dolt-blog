  How to view HUIT student portal information without logging in. Here you can look up student name, date of birth, ID card number, view grades, class schedule, and tuition fee details.
</p>

## Lookup HUIT Student Information
Access this portal link: <a href="https://sinhvien.huit.edu.vn/tra-cuu-thong-tin.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">HUIT Student Info Lookup</a>

<img class="aligncenter size-full" src="/media/2016/04/xem-diem-huit.jpg" alt="Check HUIT Grades" />

You can look up student details by filling in any 1 of the fields below alongside the **Security Captcha**:
- Student ID number
- Full Name + Date of Birth
- National ID Card Number

Then click **Search**. If credentials match, the **Search Results** table will display.

## How to View HUIT Student Grades
From the search results table above, click the **View Grades** link to view academic records and transcript results.
