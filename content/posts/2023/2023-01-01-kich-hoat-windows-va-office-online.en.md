Guide on how to self-activate Windows 10, 11 and Office online super fast and simple, completely virus-free.

- Step 1: Type PowerShell in the start search menu and select "Run as Administrator"
- Step 2: Copy and paste the following line and press Enter

```bash
irm https://get.activated.win | iex
```

- Step 3: Click on the title bar of the Microsoft Activation Script window and select the corresponding options as shown below.

<img src="/media/2023/01/active_windows_office.jpg" class="aligncenter size-full" alt="Activate Windows, Office online" />

#### 1. Permanent Windows Activation

#### 2. Activate Windows until year 2038

#### 3. Activate Windows or Office for 180 days

Press key 1, 2, or 3 correspondingly and it will automatically run and activate online.
