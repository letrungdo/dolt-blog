When downloading study materials, projects, or theses, you often encounter files that require payment: "This is a VIP document, to download this document...". While some online groups help download these files, waiting for responses can take forever. Here are **tips to download VIP documents** on websites like tailieu.vn, doc.edu.vn, luanvan.net.vn, etc.

## **Tips for Downloading VIP Documents**

Based on experience, almost all online documents are present on tailieu.vn. When searching for keywords, many sites display identical content copied from one another.

With luck, you might find a site offering free downloads. Otherwise, there is an alternative method **specifically for university students**.

Several universities have **digital libraries** integrated directly with TaiLieu.vn. For instance, Ho Chi Minh City University of Food Industry (HUFI) digital library is located at: <a href="http://thuvienso.cntp.edu.vn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">thuvienso.cntp.edu.vn</a>

The university provides each student with an account matching their student ID: **Account** and **default password** are set to **Student ID** (depending on the institution).

After logging in, you can freely download documents from tailieu.vn at no cost.
