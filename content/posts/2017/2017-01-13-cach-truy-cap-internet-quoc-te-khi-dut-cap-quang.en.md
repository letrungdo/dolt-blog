Since yesterday evening 11/01/2016, **Viettel internet users** could not access websites hosted abroad. I tried VPN extensions like Hotspot Shield Free VPN Proxy, Unlimited Free VPN - Hola, ... but unfortunately these extensions could not be downloaded either. Popular VPN sites like zpn.im were unreachable due to international hosting. Thus, **how to access international Internet during undersea cable cut** is to find VPN providers hosted locally in Vietnam, and VietPN is a stable VPN service I want to introduce in this article.

# Installation Guide for PC (**Windows**):

After installing VietPN, open the application and **log in using the account created in Step 1**:
