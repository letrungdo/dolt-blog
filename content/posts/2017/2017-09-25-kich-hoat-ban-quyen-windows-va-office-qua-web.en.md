To **activate permanent Windows and Office licenses**, there are many methods such as Active by phone or using MAK keys with remaining activations. This article will guide you on how to <a href="/kich-hoat-windows-10-online/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">activate Windows 10</a> and Office 2016 via web in the fastest and simplest way. Other versions follow similar steps.

## Activate Windows via Web

**Step 1**: Open RUN dialog (Windows + R). Type **slui 3** then enter your key and click **Next**, then click **Activate** to add the key.

If you are lucky, it will activate online immediately at this step.

**Step 2**: Type **slui 4**, a window pops up, select any country.

Copy the 9 sets of numbers shown in Step 2, go to the <a href="https://goo.gl/Trw4VB" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Windows and Office activation website</a>

Select **7 Digits** for Windows 8/8.1/10 or Office 2013/2016.

Select **6 Digits** for Windows 7 or Office 2010.

In general, select the corresponding digit count matching your Step 2 Installation ID.

<img class="aligncenter size-full" src="/media/2017/09/chon-7-digits.png" alt="Activate Windows and Office License" />

Then enter the 9 sets of numbers and click **Submit**

<img class="aligncenter size-full" src="/media/2017/09/dan-9-day-so.png" alt="" />

And here is the result: a Confirmation ID to activate as shown below.

<img class="aligncenter size-full" src="/media/2017/09/result-active-windows-office.png" alt="" />

Paste this Confirmation ID into the corresponding boxes in Step 3 and click Activate to complete.

## Activate Office License via Web

Open Word, go to File > Help/Account, select **Change product key** and enter your new key.

Then close Word and reopen it; a dialog will appear. Select Activate by phone... and copy the Step 2 ID.

The remaining steps are similar to the Windows activation process above.
